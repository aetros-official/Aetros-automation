## 3️⃣ Budgeting & Accommodation Tips  

### 🎯 3‑Step Budget Blueprint  

| Step | Action | How It Saves Money |
|------|--------|--------------------|
| **1** | **Set a Daily Spend Cap** – e.g., $40 / day for food, transport, entry fees. | Prevents overspending and makes ROI tracking easy. |
| **2** | **Leverage “Stay‑and‑Explore” Packages** – combine budget hostels with a single luxury night. | Keeps the experience premium without the full‑price stay. |
