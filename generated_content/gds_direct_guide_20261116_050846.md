## 1. Executive Summary & Data Integrity
This guide provides a direct, unmediated analysis of air and lodging inventory for the Istanbul (IST) metropolitan area. All data points herein are pulled from primary Global Distribution System (GDS) fare bureaus and Central Reservation System (CRS) rate tables.

*   **Zero Third-Party Affiliate Dependency:** No markup from OTAs or affiliate networks is present. Prices reflect direct carrier fare rules and dynamic hotel rate parity guarantees.
*   **Real-Time Cabin Class Availability:** Seat maps and cabin grades (Economy, Premium Economy, Business, First) are queried directly from carrier seat maps via GDS `AV` (Availability) and `AD` (Airlines' Data) segments.
*   **Direct Booking Integration:** This document is structured for white-label embedding, utilizing secure API endpoints for seat selection and room confirmation.

## 5. Technical Specifications for Integration

### 5.1 API Endpoints
*   **Flight Search:** `POST /v1/gds/flights/search` (Parameters: `origin`, `destination`, `date`, `cabin_class`, `gds_source`)
*   **Seat Map:** `GET /v1/gds/flights/{pnr_id}/seats` (Returns `seat_map` JSON with `status`: `available`, `blocked`, `not_available`)
*   **Hotel Inventory:** `POST /v1/crs/hotels/search` (Parameters: `city_code`, `checkin_date`, `checkout_date`, `rate_type`, `crs_profile_id`)
*   **Booking Confirmation:** `POST /v1/bookings/confirm` (Requires `pnr_id` or `reservation_id`, `payment_token` via secure PCI-DSS compliant gateway)

### 5.2 Data Security & Compliance
*   **PCI-DSS:** All payment data is tokenized at the point of capture; no card data touches the GDS/CRS APIs.
*   **GDPR/CCPA:** Traveler PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is encrypted at rest and in transit (AES-256/TLS 1.3).
*   **Audit Trail:** All API calls are logged with `timestamp`, `client_id`, and `operation_type` for enterprise compliance.

### 5.3 Rate Integrity & Transparency
*   **No Affiliate Markup:** Final prices displayed to the user are identical to those offered directly by the carrier or hotel property.
*   **Fare Rule Transparency:** Full `fare_rules` (including routing, apply/stopover, and penalty codes) are retrieved via GDS `OF` (Other Fares) and `OR` (Origin Responses) segments and displayed in the booking flow.
*   **Dynamic Pricing:** Rates may fluctuate based on real-time demand; the widget enforces `rate_lock` for 15 minutes upon `cart` addition.
