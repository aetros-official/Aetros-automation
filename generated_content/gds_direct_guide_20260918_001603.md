## 1️⃣ Executive Overview

This guide is the definitive reference for **embedding real‑time flight and hotel booking directly into your Enterprise Travel Portal**. It covers:

- **Direct carrier schedules** (Amadeus & Sabre)  
- **Live cabin‑class seat availability** (Economy, Premium, Business, First)  
- **Official CRS hotel rates** (price, taxes, fees, cancellation rules)  
- **White‑label widget integration** (no affiliate dependency)

**Goal** – Turn every search into a sale with *transparent pricing, instant confirmation, and a seamless brand experience*.

## 4️⃣ Flight Search & Booking Flow

### 4.1 Search Widget – `[GDS_FLIGHT_SEARCH_WIDGET]`

> **Insert this placeholder in any page** to render a fully functional flight search form that talks directly to Amadeus/Sabre.

```html
<div class="gds-flight-search-widget">
    [GDS_FLIGHT_SEARCH_WIDGET]
</div>
```

**Key Features**

- One‑page form: origin, destination, dates, passengers, cabin preference  
- Real‑time *flight schedule* retrieval (direct carrier)  
- Instant *seat‑availability* lookup by cabin class  
- Live pricing with taxes & fees, auto‑calculated by the GDS engine  
- Auto‑populated *fare families* (e.g., "Y – Economy Flex")  

**Integration API**

```json
POST /api/gds/flights/search
Content-Type: application/json
{
  "origin":"IST",
  "destination":"NYC",
  "departureDate":"2026-10-15",
  "returnDate":"2026-10-22",
  "passengers":{
    "adults":2,"children":0,"infants":0
  },
  "cabin":"Economy",
  "currency":"USD"
}
```

*Response* – array of flight options with `fareFamily`, `cabinClass`, `seatAvailability`, `priceBreakdown` (taxes, fees).

### 4.2 Seat Availability

- **Real‑time seat maps**: `GET /api/gds/flights/{flightId}/seats`
- Response format: `cabinClass` → `availableSeats` count + seat map image URL
- Update every **3 seconds** during a booking session (WebSocket or SSE).
