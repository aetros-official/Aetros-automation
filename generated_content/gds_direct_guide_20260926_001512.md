## 5. Compliance & Architecture Notes
1.  **Zero Affiliate Leakage:** All transactions are processed via direct carrier APIs (NDC or GDS EDIFACT) and direct hotel CRS connections. No affiliate IDs (e.g., `affiliate_id=xxxx`) are appended to URLs.
2.  **Real-Time Sync:** Inventory is polled every 30 seconds. If a seat (`J`) or room (`HTL001`) is sold, the widget must update immediately and reflect the new smallest available fare in the next class.
3.  **Fare Rules Transparency:** All GDS fare rules (Refundability, Change Fees, Stopover Penalties) must be displayed in the UI before payment processing, sourced directly from the GDS's NDS (Normative Data Service) or CRS policy engine.
