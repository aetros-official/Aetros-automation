## Data Access and Integration

Our portal is securely connected through a white-labeled integration using the provided GDS and CRS widgets. To access the Direct Airline Schedules, Live Cabin Class Availabilities, and Official Hotel CRS Rates, follow these steps:

1. Embed [GDS_FLIGHT_SEARCH_WIDGET] to enable users to search from the GDS's wide range of airline schedules, ensuring direct access to official airlines' offers and schedules.
2. Include [CRS_HOTEL_INVENTORY_WIDGET] to provide direct access to hotel inventories from Global and Local CRS.
3. Embedding these widgets allows users to see the latest cabin classes, itineraries, and hotel offers without relying on any external affiliate networks.
